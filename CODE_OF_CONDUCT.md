# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Zerotal to be a good experience for everyone, whatever
your background or level of experience. Contributors, maintainers and anyone
asking a question are all covered by this document.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a missing detail, not bad intent.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was new to Bun, to TypeScript, or to this
  framework at some point.
- Accept feedback on your work, and give it the way you would want to receive it:
  specific, about the code, and with a reason attached.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to **admin@zerotal.dev**. Reports are handled
privately, and we will not disclose the reporter's identity to the person being
reported.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith when enforcing this document may face
consequences themselves, as decided by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests
that do not follow this document, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone
whose behaviour they judge harmful.

Enforcement is proportionate: a first, minor lapse usually gets a private word,
not a ban.
